Project Overview

Overview

Paw Pilot was designed to make everyday pet care more organized and convenient by bringing pet health records, nearby pet services, and a social community for fur parents into one centralized mobile application.

Paw Pilot is a pet care application created to address the everyday needs of pet owners. Instead of managing pet information, searching for nearby services, and connecting with other fur parents across different platforms, users can access these experiences from one convenient application.

Throughout this project, I focused on creating a friendly and intuitive experience by organizing essential pet information, simplifying navigation, designing an interactive map experience, and creating a social space where fur parents can share and connect.

Design Thinking

From Everyday Pet Needs to One Simple Experience

Pet owners often manage their pets’ health information, search for nearby services, and connect with other fur parents across different platforms. Paw Pilot brings these everyday needs together into one organized and intuitive mobile experience.

The Challenge

Pet owners often have to rely on different platforms and tools to manage their pets’ health, find nearby services, and connect with other fur parents, making everyday pet care more scattered and time-consuming.

Scattered Pet Information

Pet profiles, medical records, and important health information can be difficult to keep organized and accessible.

Difficult to Find Pet Services

Finding nearby veterinary clinics, pet stores, and pet parks often requires searching through different platforms or websites.

Limited Pet Community

Pet owners have limited spaces specifically designed for sharing their pets, discovering other fur parents, and interacting around their shared interest in pet care.

The Solution

Paw Pilot brings essential pet care experiences together in one centralized mobile application.

All-in-One Pet Care

Pet profiles, medical records, nearby services, and community features are accessible within one application.

Interactive Pet Map

Users can quickly discover nearby clinics, pet stores, and pet parks through a location-based map experience.

Pet Community

A social feed allows fur parents to share pet photos and captions while discovering and interacting with posts from other pet owners.

Organized Health Records

Pet owners can view and input important medical information, making their pets' health records easier to manage.

Design Goal

Create a pet care companion that makes everyday pet ownership simpler, organized, and connected.

Every design decision from navigation and information structure to visual hierarchy and interaction was focused on reducing the effort required to manage pet care while creating a friendly experience that feels natural for fur parents.

Reflection

What I Learned

Designing Paw Pilot helped me understand how important it is to create a clear relationship between a product's features and the needs it is trying to address. Since the application brings together pet health, nearby services, and a social community, I had to think carefully about how these different experiences could coexist without making the interface feel overwhelming.

More importantly, this project taught me that a good interface is not only about creating visually appealing screens, but also about making every feature feel purposeful and easy to use. Paw Pilot strengthened my ability to turn a broad product idea into a clear, organized, and approachable experience while maintaining a consistent visual identity throughout the application.
